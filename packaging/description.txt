Manage WireGuard and AmneziaWG tunnels and clients on Linux servers.
krot creates, deletes, starts, and stops WireGuard and AmneziaWG 1.x, 2.0,
and 3.1 tunnels. It manages clients without restarting or recreating the
interface, preserving other clients' sessions and traffic.

The interface configuration is the single source of truth. krot preserves
existing configuration files and writes changes atomically. It generates
client configurations, terminal QR codes, AmneziaVPN keys, and KeeneticOS
commands, and provides JSON output and UDP diagnostic tools.

WireGuard/AmneziaWG tools, kernel support, iproute2, and systemd are external
requirements. krot does not configure NAT, forwarding, firewall rules, or
sysctl settings.
